Dynamo birthday parties

A birthday full of movement, laughter and fun.

Welcome to Dynamo Gymnastics, the kids' party venue with a difference!

Party Days & Times

Sunday 13:00-14:45 14:15-16:00 15:30-17:15

Party Cost

£225 per session! - Cost based on maximum 25 children   Please note that adults are not permitted to use any of the gym’s apparatus during the Gymnastics Fun Session.   Our Birthday Parties Include: One hour hire of the gym during which children can experience all the basics of gymnastics in the foam pits, bounce on the trampoline, balance on the beams and take part in fun team games, hide & seek and much more ​ Supervision by fully qualified British Gymnastics coaches for the duration of the session. ​ Private use of our Party Room for 45 minutes with tables & chairs provided. Please bring your own party food, soft drinks, decorations, tableware and anything else you need for your party (and we also kindly ask you to clear everything before you leave). ​ IMPORTANT INFORMATION: Your party room will be available 15 minutes before your guests finish their activities in the gymnastics hall. Gymnastics Fun Sessions are popular, so don’t delay your booking.​
