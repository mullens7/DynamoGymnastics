Pre-School Birthday Parties!

Party Days & Times

Saturday 14:30 - 15:30

Children 1-4yrs  Book your child a pre-school party! In brilliant facilities! Each party costs £95 which includes:

The hire of the pre-school gym for 1 hour where the children can explore gymnastics on our soft play equipment, bounce on the trampoline, and use all our interesting hand apparatus! - Also fun songs! - Supervised by our fully qualified British Gymnastics coaches for the duration of the party. - You may have a maximum of 15 people attend your pre-school party. - Birthday child will receive a dynamo t-shirt and certificate! ​ Party Rules: ​ 1) NO food or drink is permitted in the pre-school gymnastics hall. 2) Please inform coaching staff of any condition that may affect a child's ability to participate safely 3) Any damage to the equipment must be reported. 4) Adults are not permitted to use the apparatus but are required to stay and supervise the children.
