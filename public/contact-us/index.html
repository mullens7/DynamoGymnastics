Classes & enquiries

A little information before you join us.

How joining works

Our Hamble Gyminis and Recreational sessions cost £10 per session. When a space becomes available, we’ll invite your child to a one-off paid taster session (£10) so they can see whether they enjoy it.

If you decide to take the place, you’ll pay by monthly Direct Debit through Thrive4 on the 1st of each month, for the sessions in the month ahead.

Hamble

Classes run all year round, except over the Christmas period. You won’t be charged for sessions during these closures.

West End

West End bookings and waiting lists are managed separately by Gymtots. Please visit gym-tots.co.uk to book or join their waiting list.

Current waiting times

GyminisApproximately 3–6 monthsWe’ll do our best to offer a space as soon as possible.

RecreationalImmediately to around 1–2 monthsAvailability depends on your preferred classes.

Waiting times are estimates and a place isn’t guaranteed.
