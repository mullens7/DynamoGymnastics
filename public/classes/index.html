Gymtots

Little movements, big adventures!

Dynamo's Gymtots offers a fantastic range of fun, engaging gymnastics classes for children under five, held at our Hamble facility and community venues across the local area.

Our classes give little ones the opportunity to climb, roll, jump, balance and explore in a safe, welcoming and exciting environment. Using specially designed soft play equipment, children develop essential movement skills, coordination, balance and confidence, all whilst having lots of fun!

Something for every little gymnast

Our age-appropriate programmes are carefully designed to support children at each stage of their early development, with dedicated classes for babies, two-year-olds and children aged three to four.

Through a combination of basic gymnastics skills, imaginative play, action rhymes, music and exciting themed activities, children are encouraged to stay active, build confidence, develop social skills and make new friends.

Learning, growing and having fun!

All Gymtots sessions are delivered by qualified British Gymnastics coaches and follow fundamental movement programmes accredited by British Gymnastics, the UK's national governing body for the sport.

With exciting themes, badges, achievements and rewards to celebrate progress, there's always something new to discover and plenty to keep our youngest gymnasts smiling.

Big smiles, little achievements and lots of fun — that's what Gymtots is all about!
