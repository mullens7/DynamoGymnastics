Gym Tots · Under 5s

Dynamo's Gym Tots runs a variety of fun gym classes for children under 5yrs old from our premises in Hamble and centres in the local community. Children love to climb, roll, jump and leap and with the use of soft play apparatus, Gym Tots classes promote basic movement skills, co-ordination and balance in a safe and fun environment. The structured physical play programmes are designed according to age groups (Baby Gym, 2yrs old, 3-4yrs old) and aim to encourage children to enjoy physical activity. Basic gymnastics skills, action rhymes and songs plus the development of socially acceptable behaviour are incorporated into a whole range of activities based around various themes within each age group. Gym Tots classes are run by fully qualified British Gymnastics coaches. Classes run by Gym Tots follow the Fundamental movement programmes accredited by British Gymnastics, the UK national governing body for gymnastics. Exciting themes… badges… rewards to keep children motivated. Loads of FUN guaranteed !

Gyminis · Ages 4–7

Gyminis classes are for girls and boys aged between 4 and 7 years. Classes are undertaken in a fun but structured environment and include the use of a variety of apparatus to teach girls and boys basic gymnastic skills. Gyminis covers floor, beam, vault, trampoline as well as conditoning and flexibility. Classes give children the opportunity to work towards Dynamo Gymnastics’ badge system, under the supervision of one of our qualified coaches. Coaches are always monitoring the gymnasts to identify a potential to move them into our talent identification programme or an appropriate squad.

For Hamble class enquiries, include your child’s name, age and preferred class.

Recreational · Ages 7+

The Recreational Gymnastics Classes welcome children of 7 years and over and invite young gymnasts to use the gym’s full range of apparatus in our mixed classes. We use the British Gymnastics Badge Scheme which helps to provide a measure of the children’s progress and we offer a full scheme for general gymnastics, floor, vault and trampoline. All gymnasts are also offered the opportunity to take part in Fun Competitions and anyone showing potential may be selected for our Performance Squads.

Stay & Play

Stay and Play runs every Saturday morning at The Hamble Sports Complex from 8:15am to 9:10am. It’s £7.50 per child, members and non-members welcome. No need to book and no commitments - simply turn up and pay on the door for a fun, free-play session in the gym.

Adult gymnastics

The Adult Gymnastics classes are mixed ability groups for anyone over the age of 18 who is interested in the sport. The classes feature a 10-15min coach led warm up including cardio vascular exercises followed by general work on strength and flexibility. For the remainder of the session, participants are free to use the various apparatus in the gym and the lead coach is always on hand to advise and assist in any specific skill learning.
